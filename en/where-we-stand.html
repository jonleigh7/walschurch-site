What we believe

Where we stand

These are our positions on questions people often ask about. Each one comes from our reading of Scripture, and the passages are listed so you can read them yourself.

  • Sexuality
  • Gender
  • Racism and oppression
  • Abortion
  • ← Mission and beliefs

Sexuality

We believe that God's moral commandments in the Old Testament still apply under our new covenant through Jesus Christ, and that we must obey them. At ABOVE, we believe that all acts of sexuality outside the boundaries of God's commands and creation are sin, including bisexuality, homosexuality, adultery, fornication, polygamy, orgies, bestiality, pornography, and sex before marriage. (Leviticus 18:22; Leviticus 20:13; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Ephesians 5:3; Matthew 19:4-6; Exodus 20:14; Deuteronomy 22:5; Exodus 22:19) We believe God sees all sexual immorality equally as sin. No one sexual sin is greater than another.

We understand that we all face temptation, just as Jesus Christ was tempted. (Matthew 4:1-11) Many Christians are tempted with sexually immoral desires. We do not believe that having these temptations in our minds is a sin. They are strongholds and demonic attacks on our minds, meant to get us to act in sin. We believe these temptations are overcome through the renewing of our minds, which leads to freedom and transformation in our lives from sexual immorality and temptation.

Gender

We believe that God's creation and intention for mankind is binary: male and female. (Genesis 1:27; Deuteronomy 22:5; Matthew 19:4) God was purposeful when He created man and woman. He created two genders with significantly different biological functions and outward differences. Men are not capable of becoming pregnant. Only women are capable of getting pregnant and giving birth. This is God's absolute truth in His creation and design for all mankind.

We don't believe in multiple truths. We believe in one truth, which is God's absolute and infallible truth. We don't believe feelings determine what is right or moral, or our gender identity, because feelings are fluid and they change. We believe that only God's absolute truth can determine our gender identity and what is right and moral. Anything that thwarts God's creation and is outside His truth and intentions is a lie and an attack from Satan, trying to confuse the gender identity God created for us from birth.

Racism and oppression

We look at social issues only through the lens of God and not of this world. We stand against every form of racism and oppression toward any group of people. When we see true forms of systemic racism and oppression, we will stand against them. We do not believe one race or group of people is the sole cause of all racism and oppression. Racism and oppression are a worldwide issue of the individual heart. It is not only a black and white issue, as the worldly media constantly emphasizes. Oppression and slavery still happen in the world today, even within communities of the same race and ethnicity in many countries. We don't believe there is any current form of racial supremacy or systemic racism in the United States. We don't believe systemic oppression is current today in the United States.

We are a community that is not quick to judge or blame based on what we first see. We believe each of us has a mind of our own to make judgments based on careful research for truth, data and statistics, and listening to the perspectives of all views and voices. Here at ABOVE, we do not fight for social issues according to the world's standards, and we do not give in to the pressure of the world's view on social issues. We stand on God's Word and His standard to deal with and act on these issues, especially here in the United States.

At ABOVE, we believe all governmental authority is ordained by God. (Romans 13:1-2) Honor and submission to them is a command from God. We honor and submit to all governmental leaders in political office. We honor and submit to all police. We bless all authority with our words and actions. We believe we can change legislation honorably through the authority we have in the United States: voting, freedom of speech, and peaceful protest. Most importantly, we honor all authority through prayer and intercession. We are grateful to live in a free country. We don't believe the United States is a perfect country; it has its flaws. But we believe that through fervent prayer and supplication, God will transform this nation according to His ways.

Abortion

We believe that all forms of murder are sin, especially the murder of an innocent child in or outside of the womb. We believe that God has a plan and purpose for every life. God is about giving life, and life more abundantly. Every child is a creation of God. We believe that God knows each of us before and while we are in the womb, and that every life was already planned out by God before conception. We believe that life begins at conception. (Exodus 20:13; Psalm 139:13-16; Jeremiah 1:5; Psalm 127:3-5; Job 31:15; Psalm 22:10; Isaiah 49:15)

We believe that every unborn life deserves a chance to live. As much as we value the baby's life, we value the mother's life equally. We support all mothers who have dealt with domestic abuse and trauma. We support all mothers who are struggling financially. We support all young mothers who are pursuing a career. We understand that many different circumstances lead to a woman becoming pregnant. When this comes up in our community, we will gladly point any mother facing an unplanned pregnancy to resources, Christian adoption agencies, and help.

We believe that every woman and every person has the right to do what they want with their own body, as long as it does not affect or harm the lives of others. In this case, we believe babies and unborn lives also have a right to their lives. We don't believe a baby or unborn life deserves punishment for the violent crimes of the father or for the parents' convenience. We stand up for every human life, just as God does.
