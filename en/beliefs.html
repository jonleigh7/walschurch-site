What we believe

Mission & beliefs

Our mission

There is a desire in our hearts to see young people rising up now to become strong and courageous leaders for Jesus, bringing the kingdom of heaven to a broken and dark world.

We believe we are called to be the image and likeness of Jesus: to act, speak, love, and think like Him.

Our mission is to renew the minds of youth and young adults with God's Word, so we see godly transformation and revival in the lives of a lost and confused generation.

Statement of faith

We believe

  1. The Bible is the only inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, for salvation, doctrine, reproof, correction, instruction in righteousness, and equipping.

    2 Tim. 3:16-17

  2. In the deity of our Lord Jesus Christ, in His virgin birth, in His sinless life, in His miracles, in His atoning death through His shed blood, in His bodily resurrection, in His ascension to Heaven, and in His personal return to earth in great power and glory to rule over the nations.

    John 10:30; 14:9; Matt. 1:18-25; John 20:30-31; Heb. 4:15; 7:26; 9:11-15; Luke 24:36-48; Acts 2:30-33; 1:9-11; 1 Thess. 4:16

  3. Man was created in God's image, but sinned and was separated from God, and can be redeemed only through Jesus Christ.

    Genesis 1:26-31; 2:17; 3:6,17; Romans 5:12-21

  4. The grace of God makes salvation available to all. Through the preaching of repentance, man can be renewed by the Holy Spirit, justified by grace through faith, and become a new creation, an heir of God.

    Titus 2:11; 3:5-7; Romans 10:13-15; Luke 24:47; Eph. 2:8-9

  5. The body of Christ consists of all believers in Jesus Christ.

    1 Cor. 12:12-13

  6. The local church is designed and established by God as the primary place of worship, instruction, fellowship, and evangelism.

    Acts 2:42-47

  7. All believers are indwelled by the Holy Spirit, who comforts, sanctifies, and enables them to live a godly life.

    1 Cor. 6:19; John 14:16,26

  8. All believers are entitled to the Baptism of the Holy Spirit by Jesus Christ, with the initial biblical evidence of speaking in tongues. The promise has been made to each believer. Our responsibility is to ask for and receive the baptism, according to the command of Jesus Christ. The manifestation of spiritual power that follows in public testimony and service, and a holy life, is further evidence of this baptism.

    Luke 24:49; Acts 1:4-8; 2:4,38-39; 10:44-46; 11:14-16; 15:7-9; 19:2-6

  9. All who repent and believe on Christ as Savior are commanded by Scripture to be baptized in water, declaring to the world their identification with the death and resurrection of Christ and the newness of life within.

    Matthew 28:19-20; Acts 10:47-48; Romans 6:4

  10. Christ's death on the cross provided total redemption for the human spirit, soul, and body.

    Matt. 8:17; Isaiah 53:4-5; Mark 16:18; John 5:14

  11. In the return of the Lord Jesus Christ and the resurrection of both the saved and the lost: the saved unto eternal life, living eternally in the presence of God, and the lost unto eternal damnation without further opportunity of repentance.

    1 Cor. 15; 1 Thess. 4:13-18; Revelation 22:20; John 14:3; Revelation 20:11-15
