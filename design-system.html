Design system

The tokens and components behind the church site. Everything here lives in assets/css/system.css.

Open the site

Principles

Warm and colorful. The palette comes from the church itself: the plaster wall in the portraits, terracotta, morning gold, and deep green. Whole sections take a color so the page has rhythm as you scroll.

Two languages, equal weight. Both fonts were chosen because they render Vietnamese diacritics properly. English and Vietnamese pages share every component.

Photos of real people. Use photos from church life instead of stock images. Portraits are cropped 4:5, scenes 16:9 or 3:2.

Plain words. Write the way a pastor would talk to a visitor at the door. Headings don't end in periods. Labels are short italics, never spaced-out capitals.

Color

Sections alternate between light (paper, stone, sun) and dark (forest, clay, forest deep). On dark sections, text turns cream and accents turn gold. Section classes: .section--stone, --sun, --sky, --forest, --clay, --dark.

Type

EB Garamond for headings, labels, quotes, and Scripture. It's a Garamond in the tradition of printed Bibles and hymnals, and its italic carries every label on the site.

Literata for reading text and buttons. A book serif made for long reading on screens, with full Vietnamese support so stacked marks like ộ and ữ stay clear.
